# H1-03 · The 5/10 rule from a bay spawn

- **IF/THEN:** IF a new player spawns in a free bay with their ball already on the tee and one target highlighted with its points and an arrow above it, THEN at least 4 of 5 first-time players take their first useful action within 5 seconds and can state what they are trying to do by 10 seconds, without help.
- **Source section:** §2 First Minutes — the 0–5 and 5–10 second rows
- **Cheapest killing test:** greybox in desktop Explorer, 3 to 5 people who have not seen it, ~30 min to build
- **Key metric:** share of first-time players acting within 5 seconds; under 80% counts as failure
- **Mobile-sensitive:** yes
- **Tested on:** —
- **Parked:** 2026-09-18
